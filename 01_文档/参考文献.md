# 参考文献 / 数据来源

## Stanford Drone Dataset (SDD)
- **论文**: Robicquet A, Sadeghian A, Alahi A, Savarese S. *Learning Social Etiquette: Human Trajectory Understanding in Crowded Scenes*[C]. European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2016.
- **官方站**: https://cvgl.stanford.edu/projects/uav_data/
- **标注镜像**: https://github.com/flclain/StanfordDroneDataset

## ETH 行人数据集 (ETH Walking Pedestrians)
- **论文**: Pellegrini S, Ess A, Schindler K, Van Gool L. *You'll Never Walk Alone: Modeling Social Behavior for Multi-Target Tracking*[C]. IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 2009.

## UCY 行人数据集
- **论文**: Lerner A, Chrysanthou Y, Lischinski D. *Crowds by Example*[J]. Computer Graphics Forum, 26(3), 2007.

## ETH/UCY 原始数据(世界坐标)来源
- **仓库**: https://github.com/StanfordASL/Trajectron-plus-plus （`experiments/pedestrians/raw/raw/all_data/`）
